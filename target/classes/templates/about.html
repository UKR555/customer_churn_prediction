About This Project

Grocery Customer Churn Prediction

This application uses machine learning to predict whether grocery store customers are likely to churn (stop shopping at your store) based on their demographics and shopping behavior.

Project Overview

Customer churn is a significant challenge for grocery retailers. When customers leave to shop elsewhere, it results in lost revenue and increased marketing costs to acquire new customers. Identifying customers who are likely to churn before they leave gives retailers the opportunity to intervene with targeted retention strategies.

Machine Learning Models

This application evaluates several machine learning algorithms and automatically selects the highest-performing model for making predictions. The models evaluated include:

  • Decision Tree (J48)
  • Random Forest
  • Naive Bayes
  • Logistic Regression
  • Support Vector Machine (SMO)

Data Features

The model makes predictions based on a variety of customer features, including:

  • Demographics (age, gender, income)
  • Shopping frequency
  • Average basket value
  • Loyalty program membership
  • Online shopping usage
  • Days since last purchase
  • Total purchase amount
  • Store visit frequency
  • Complaints made
  • Special offers redeemed
  • Customer feedback scores
  • Preferred payment method and store location

How to Use

  1. Navigate to the Predict page
  2. Enter the customer's demographic and shopping behavior information
  3. Click "Predict Churn" to see the prediction results
  4. Review the prediction outcome and recommended retention actions for at-risk customers
